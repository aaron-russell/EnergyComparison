# Compare energy tariffs using past household usage

Energy Replay lets you compare tariff definitions against the same selected household readings
and dates. It is a historical calculation: rates you enter are applied to past usage so you can
inspect how the totals and monthly costs differ.

## What you need

- Half-hourly electricity and, if relevant, gas readings for a period you want to review.
- Tariff rates and standing charges to compare, entered by you or loaded from a tariff file.
- Enough coverage to make the period useful. The app shows missing intervals and any estimates
  separately so you can decide how to interpret them.

You can use synthetic examples to explore the workflow without connecting an account or importing
your household data. [Open Energy Replay](https://energy.russell-tech.co.uk/app/).

## How the replay works

Choose a baseline tariff and one or more alternatives. The application prices the same supply,
dates, and energy against each tariff, then shows period and monthly costs with breakdowns by fuel,
standing charge, and time band where applicable. Decimal arithmetic is used for money and energy.

For an annual comparison, select a complete 12-month period. A shorter or incomplete period is
shown as a period total, not represented as a full-year outcome. Rates entered today are replayed
unchanged over the historical dates.

## What the result does not tell you

The replay does not predict future bills, find live supplier offers, rank suppliers, recommend a
tariff, or guarantee savings. It does not account for future changes in usage or rates. The result
depends on the readings, tariff definitions, date range, and treatment of missing data you select.

Before relying on a result, review [coverage and estimates](/guide/coverage-and-estimates), the
[calculation assumptions](/calculations), and the [full app walkthrough](/guide/using-the-app).
