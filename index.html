Private by design

Your energy history stays in your session

Imported readings, connection credentials, and comparison progress are held in the browser tab session. Credentials and live connections are not saved. Only tariff definitions you explicitly save and your theme preference use local storage.

Know what the result means

A historical replay, not a forecast

Energy Replay prices past usage against the tariff definitions you provide. It does not predict future bills, fetch a live supplier catalogue, rank suppliers, provide energy advice, or guarantee savings. Results depend on the data, dates, and rates entered.
